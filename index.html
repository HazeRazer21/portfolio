01 / Thesis GMM-HMM Nested Walk-Forward

Automated Overfitting Detection for Trading Strategies

Academic thesis, 2026

Problem
Manual overfitting checks miss strategies that only look good in backtests, and a strategy that passes review can still lose capital.
Approach
A hybrid GMM-HMM model reads market regimes, paired with Nested Walk-Forward Analysis to test the strategy across time.
Result
A diagnostic framework that identifies overfitting more accurately and systematically, so strategy evaluation is more reliable before deployment.
Stack
Python, GMM, HMM, statistical analysis, time-series data
